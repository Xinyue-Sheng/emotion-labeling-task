🏷️ Tweet Emotion Labeling Task

A short annotation task · ~3 minutes

What you'll do

You will read 5 short tweets and, for each one, choose the emotion you think the author was expressing. Every participant is shown a different, randomly chosen set of 5 tweets drawn from a pool of 120 tweets taken from the public dair-ai/emotion dataset.

  • For each tweet, pick exactly one of six emotion categories: anger, fear, joy, love, sadness, or surprise.
  • Go with your first instinct — there are no trick questions and no "correct" answer we're looking for.
  • Your responses are recorded along with your name/ID and a timestamp, purely for this class assignment.
  • The task takes about 2–3 minutes.
